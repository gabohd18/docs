A restaurant runs differently from a coffee shop: tables stay open during service, the kitchen gets a stream of orders coming in and going out, guests split the bill, and tips are settled per shift. El Nido handles all of it from your phone, with nothing to install.

## How a restaurant uses El Nido

A Thursday night service:

- The server takes the order from their phone and sends it to an **account** named after the table.
- The order lands on the **kitchen screen** with its time. The cook marks it **preparing**, then **ready**.
- Every time the table orders more, it goes on the same account.
- At the end, if each guest pays their own share, you **split the bill** and charge by parts.
- The tip is captured at checkout and **accumulated per shift**.
- At close, the **shift cut** reconciles everything from that shift.

## When you don't need a POS for your restaurant

- You sell fewer than 30 tickets a day.
- You run the floor solo, with no servers.
- You don't care about reconciling tips or measuring profitability.

At low volume, a notebook still works. A POS starts to pay off when you have several servers, a kitchen that gets slammed, or you want to know what you're really making.
