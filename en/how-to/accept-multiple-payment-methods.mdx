It is very common for a customer to pay part in cash and part by card. Or cash plus transfer. Or voucher plus cash. If your POS only allows one payment method per ticket, you end up creating two separate tickets for one sale, and your reports come out wrong.

## The right flow

1. Build the ticket as usual (products, modifiers, tip if applicable).
2. Before charging, tap "Split payment".
3. Enter the first method and amount. Example: $200 cash.
4. The system automatically calculates what is missing.
5. Enter the second method. Example: $150 by card via Clip.
6. If something is still owed, add a third (transfer, voucher, store credit).
7. Close the ticket. It prints with each method's breakdown.

## Methods El Nido recognizes

| Method | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Cash** | With automatic change calculation |
| **Card (external terminal)** | Clip, Mercado Pago, Banca, any. You enter the amount manually |
| **Transfer / SPEI** | Just enter the amount, proof goes by WhatsApp |
| **Voucher / courtesy** | For exchanges, gifts or errors |
| **Customer balance** | If the customer has credit on their profile |
| **Store credit (fiado)** | Recorded against the customer profile to collect later |

## Tips split by method

If the ticket carries a tip and the customer pays with two methods, El Nido lets you assign the tip to the method you prefer. Example: subtotal in cash, tip on card so it shows on the customer's statement.

Useful when the customer wants to leave the tip by card so it counts as a deductible expense.

## Common cases

**Customer wanted to pay by card but it got rejected.**
No drama. Cancel that method and record cash. The ticket does not reopen, you just change the method before closing.

**Customer overpaid in cash.**
The system shows the change. If they want to leave it as tip, you record it as tip before closing the ticket.

**Terminal balance shortfall and customer covers the rest in cash.**
You record what went through on card and the rest in cash. Total reconciled.

## When you do NOT need multi-payment

- You take cash 100% of the time.
- Your customers never split their payment.
- You do not accept cards.

But if you sell in a plaza, café or restaurant, multi-payment becomes a need fast.
