When a business grows to its second location, most POS systems break. They either force you to configure each location as a separate system (no consolidated reports) or charge you enterprise for multi-branch. El Nido handles it natively from the multi-location plan.

## How to configure a second location

1. From the business dashboard, go to Locations → New.
2. Name and address it. Example: "Plaza Mayor location".
3. Decide whether it shares catalog with the main one or has its own.
4. Add users with permissions per location.
5. Location ready to sell.

## Catalog models

| Model | When to use |
|---|---|
| **100% shared** | Same menu, same prices across locations. Change price at main and it updates everywhere |
| **Independent** | Each location has its own menu. Useful when formats differ (small vs large location) |
| **Hybrid** | Core products shared, local products per location. Most cases fall here |

## Consolidated reports

From the owner app you see all locations on one dashboard:

- Day sales consolidated + per location.
- Top product global + top product per location.
- Comparison between locations (which sells more, which has better margin).
- Inventory per location with differences.
- Active employees per location.

## Multi-terminal per location

A single location can have several registers operating at once:

- 2 registers at a restaurant entrance.
- 1 bar register + 1 patio register at a café.
- 3 checkout points at a bakery during peak hour.

Each terminal identifies with a name and operates independently, but all push to the same backend. Reports show what each terminal sold by shift.

## Inter-location transfers

When one location needs product from another (example: main sends bread to satellite), you log the transfer:

1. Origin location marks products to transfer.
2. Destination location confirms receipt.
3. Inventory adjusts on both sides.
4. Date, products and quantities stay on record.

Useful when you manage centralized inventory and distribute to locations.

## When you do NOT care about multi-location

- You have a single location and do not plan to grow.
- Your spots are so different each one is a separate business.
- You do not care about consolidating reports.

If you only run one, the Lite plan is enough. Multi-location applies from the second location onward.
