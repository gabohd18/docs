Yes, there are free POS systems in Mexico. But "free" can mean three very different things, and only one of them works for a serious small business.

## The 3 ways a POS claims to be free

1. **Free with commission per sale.** They give you the software at no monthly cost, but charge 1-3% on every transaction. If you sell $50,000 MXN/month, that is $500 to $1,500 in commission every month. Not free.
2. **Free for a limited time (trial).** They give you 14 or 30 days at no cost, then you pay or it shuts off. Useful to test, not a free plan.
3. **Permanent Free plan with limits.** They let you run forever at no cost in a trimmed version (fewer products, one location, basic reports). This is the only model you can honestly call "free" to get started.

## What a serious free plan must include

To actually be useful without paying:

- Unlimited sales (not "20 tickets per month")
- Product catalog without artificially low caps
- Access to your basic data (how much you sold, what sold)
- Zero commission per sale
- No credit card required to activate

If they ask for a card to "activate your free trial", it is not free. It is a trial in disguise.

## When the Free plan is enough

- Single-person or couple-run business.
- Under 50 products in catalog.
- One location only.
- No CFDI invoicing needed yet.
- Cash and card sales without complex tipping.

## When you should pay

- You are growing (more than one location, employees taking shifts).
- You need CFDI 4.0 automatic on every ticket.
- You want recipes with profit calculation per product.
- You run a restaurant or café and need KDS for the kitchen.
- You want Pío AI to ask your business questions instead of reconciling reports.

## When the Free plan does NOT cut it

- You manage complex inventory with shared ingredients.
- You sell from several physical points or employees rotate shifts.
- Your customers expect automatic invoicing.
- You want retention or cohort analytics.
