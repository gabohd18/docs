Pío is the conversational assistant inside El Nido. It is not a generic chatbot. Pío knows your sales, products, customers and cash drawer. You ask in plain language and it answers with real numbers pulled from your own data.

## What you can ask

Real questions customers ask every day:

- "How much did I sell this week compared to last?"
- "What sells most on Sundays?"
- "Show me my 10 most loyal customers."
- "Which product brings the most margin?"
- "How much do I have in the cash drawer right now?"
- "Build a promo to move stuck inventory."
- "Why did sales drop in August?"
- "Which hour of the morning is busiest?"
- "Which employees sell the most?"

Pío understands natural language. You do not have to learn commands or write SQL. You speak to it like you would speak to an accountant who knows your business.

## How it works

1. You open Pío's chat from the app.
2. You type your question in plain language.
3. Pío analyzes your live data (sales, products, customers, inventory, cash drawer).
4. It answers with concrete numbers and, when it applies, recommendations.

All inside the app. No need to export to Excel or learn BI tools.

## What Pío does NOT do yet

To stay honest:

- **It does not execute actions for you.** Pío tells you what to do, but changes (raising prices, modifying inventory, sending promos) you approve.
- **It does not predict the future.** It tells you what happened and why, not what tomorrow will look like with certainty.
- **It does not replace a certified accountant.** It shows you operating profit, not tax filings.
- **It does not know data outside El Nido.** If you pay commissions to Uber Eats, Pío does not see them unless you record them.
