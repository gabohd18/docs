Pío is El Nido's conversational assistant. It's not a generic chatbot. Pío knows your sales, your products, your customers, and your cash register. You ask a question in plain language and it answers with real numbers, pulled from your own data.

## What you can ask it

A few real questions customers ask every day:

- "How much did I sell this week compared to last week?"
- "What sells best on Sundays?"
- "Show me my 10 most loyal customers."
- "Which product makes the most profit?"
- "How much is in the register right now?"
- "Put together a promo to move slow inventory."
- "Why did sales drop in August?"
- "Which morning hour is the busiest?"
- "Which employees sell the most?"

Pío understands natural language. You don't have to learn commands or write SQL. You talk to it the way you'd talk to an accountant who knows your business.

## How it works

1. You open Pío's chat from the app.
2. You type your question in plain language.
3. Pío analyzes your live data (sales, products, customers, inventory, cash).
4. It answers with concrete numbers and, when it applies, recommendations.

All inside the app. No exporting to Excel and no BI tools to learn.

## What Pío does not do yet

To keep it honest:

- **It doesn't take actions for you.** Pío tells you what to do, but the changes (raising prices, editing inventory, sending promos) are yours to approve.
- **It doesn't predict the future.** It tells you what happened and why, not what will happen tomorrow with certainty.
- **It doesn't replace a certified accountant.** It shows you your operating profit, it doesn't file your taxes.
- **It doesn't know data outside El Nido.** If you pay commissions to Uber Eats, Pío doesn't see them unless you record them.

## Privacy

Your data is never used to train external AI models. Pío uses Google Gemini Flash in private mode: your sales, customers, and transactions stay in your account. Every answer is generated by querying only your own data, not that of other businesses.
