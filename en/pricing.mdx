El Nido charges a monthly or annual subscription. **Zero commission per sale**, no matter how much you sell. No contract. No penalty for leaving.

## Plan comparison

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Who it is for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | $0 | New business, 1 location, small catalog |
| **Lite** | MXN $349 | MXN $3,490 (~$291/month equivalent) | Operating business, 1 location |
| **Business** | MXN $999 | MXN $9,990 (~$832/month equivalent) | Full operation with AI assistant |
| **Multi-location** | from MXN $1,499 | from MXN $14,990 | 2 or more locations |

## What each plan includes

### Free plan ($0/month)

- Unlimited sales
- Product catalog without cap
- 1 location, 1 user
- Basic digital points card (stamps mechanic)
- Basic reports (day sales, top products)
- Multi-method payments
- No commission per sale, no credit card required

### Lite plan ($349/month)

Everything in Free, plus:
- Up to 3 users (cashiers)
- Recipes with cost per ingredient
- Low-stock alerts
- CFDI 4.0 automatic, no cost per stamp
- Self-service invoicing via QR
- Reports by category, hour, employee
- Unlimited variants and modifiers
- Combos as their own entity
- WhatsApp support

### Business plan ($999/month)

Everything in Lite, plus:
- Unlimited users
- Kitchen / barista KDS
- Table map with multi-server
- Tips split by shift
- Pío AI Assistant (natural-language questions to your business)
- Multi-tier digital card (points, stamps, tiers, cashback)
- Native Apple Wallet and Google Pay
- Multi-terminal per location
- Excel and accountant export
- Priority support

### Multi-location plan (from $1,499/month)

Everything in Business, plus:
- 2 or more locations with shared or independent catalog
- Consolidated reports across locations
- Inter-location transfers
- Roles and permissions per location
- Performance comparison between locations

Each additional location adds with declining price.

## Zero commission per sale. Forever.

Unlike other POS systems in Mexico, El Nido does not take a percentage of your sales. Whether you sell $50,000, $200,000 or $2,000,000 a month, your subscription stays the same.

If your current POS charges 1-3% per sale, you are paying MXN $500 to $60,000 per month in hidden fees. With El Nido that money stays with you.

## The 14-day Business plan trial

You get **14 days free of the full Business plan**:
- No credit card required
- No contract
- No upgrade commitment at the end
- If it is not for you, drop to the Free plan and keep operating without paying

On day 60, if you do nothing, you auto-drop to Free. No surprises on your statement.

## FAQ

**Do you charge a setup fee?**
No. Zero implementation cost. 2-minute setup on your own.

**Do you charge per additional user?**
On Lite there is a 3-user cap. On Business and Multi-location it is unlimited at no extra charge.

**Do you charge per CFDI stamp?**
No. CFDI 4.0 invoicing is included in Lite and Business at no additional cost.

**Do you charge for additional terminals in a location?**
On Business and Multi-location, multi-terminal is included. You can run 2, 3, 5 registers in the same location at no extra cost.

**Is there a discount for annual payment?**
Yes, equivalent to 2 free months (pay 10, use 12).

**Do you accept external terminals (Clip, Mercado Pago, BBVA)?**
Yes, any external terminal. El Nido does not force you into branded hardware.

**Do I need to buy specific hardware?**
No. El Nido runs from any phone, tablet or computer browser with internet.
