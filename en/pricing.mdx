El Nido charges a monthly or annual subscription. **Zero per-sale commissions**, no matter how much you sell. No contract, and no penalty if you cancel.

## Plan comparison

| Plan | Monthly | Annual (2 months free) | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | $0 | A new business getting started |
| **Starter** | $349 MXN | $3,490 MXN | A running business that wants basic loyalty |
| **Pro** | $699 MXN | $6,990 MXN | An established business that wants to see its profitability |
| **Business** | $999 MXN | $9,990 MXN | A full operation with an AI assistant |

## What each plan includes

### Free ($0/mo)

- 50 products, 1 user
- Point of sale, inventory, and cash register
- Basic dashboard (daily sales, top products)
- Public profile on the Hub
- 14 days of history

### Starter ($349/mo)

Everything in Free, plus:
- 150 products, 2 users
- **Digital points card** (basic loyalty)
- Credit accounts (running tabs)
- Waste tracking
- Staff scheduling
- 60 days of history

### Pro ($699/mo)

Everything in Starter, plus:
- 300 products, 5 users
- **Live income statement** (P&L, cash flow, and margins)
- Inventory counts, purchase orders, and suppliers
- Advanced analytics and monthly goals
- QR orders and order tracking
- **Digital points card** with tiers and promotions
- **Apple Wallet and Google Pay**
- 90 days of history

### Business ($999/mo)

Everything in Pro, plus:
- Unlimited products and users
- **Pío**, your AI assistant (ask your business questions in plain language)
- Full loyalty: cashback and referrals
- Custom branding on your cards
- Priority support and a featured spot on the Hub
- Unlimited history

## Just want loyalty, without the point of sale?

There are **loyalty-only** plans for businesses that already have a POS and only want the digital points card:

- **Loyalty Starter** ($299/mo): 500 members
- **Loyalty Pro** ($499/mo): 2,000 members plus Apple Wallet and Google Pay
- **Loyalty Business** ($799/mo): unlimited members

## No per-sale commissions. Ever.

Unlike other POS systems in Mexico, El Nido does not take a percentage of your sales. Whether you sell $50,000 a month, $200,000, or $2,000,000, your subscription is the same. What many POS systems keep as a commission on every sale, with El Nido you keep for yourself.

## The 14-day Business trial

We give you **14 days free of the full Business plan**:
- No credit card
- No contract and no obligation to upgrade
- If it's not for you, you stay on Free and keep operating without paying

On day 14, if you do nothing, you drop automatically to Free. No surprises on your statement.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is there an installation fee?**
No. Zero setup fee. You sign up yourself in 2 minutes.

**Do you charge for each additional user?**
Each plan comes with its own number of users (Free 1, Starter 2, Pro 5). On Business they're unlimited.

**Is there a discount for paying annually?**
Yes. The annual price is the same as 2 months free: you pay for 10 months and use 12.

**Do you accept external terminals (Clip, Mercado Pago, bank terminals)?**
Yes, any external terminal. El Nido does not lock you into a branded terminal.

**Do I have to buy specific hardware?**
No. El Nido runs in the browser on any phone, tablet, or computer with internet.

**What about tax invoicing (CFDI)?**
Automatic CFDI invoicing is coming in 2026. It's not available yet.
